# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery. Items must be unused and returned in their original packaging. Once an eligible return is received and inspected, the refund is sent to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Digital software licenses are refundable within 14 calendar days only if the license has not been activated. Shipping fees are not refundable unless the return is caused by an incorrect or damaged item. Customers should contact support with their order number to start a return.
